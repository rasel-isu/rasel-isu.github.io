Findings of EMNLP 2026

Bridging Lexical Divergence: Zero-shot Scientific Entity Linking

In scientific text, a mention and the ontology entity it refers to often share no words (“lambing potential” → Female Fecundity Trait). Sci-ZSEL uses an LLM to write aliases for a small, selected set of entities, filters aliases that drift toward neighboring concepts using the ontology hierarchy, and fine-tunes a retriever and reranker on the resulting pseudo-pairs, with no human labels.

+49.1Recall@1 pts on no-overlap mentions (LPT)
5benchmarks, including 3 new animal-science sets
0human-annotated training pairs

01 / Medical AI safety

Multi-agent evaluation loops

Generator LLMs answer clinical queries while evaluator LLMs score them against AMA medical-ethics principles and a five-tier risk protocol, looping until the answer is safe. Over 900 queries, the loop cut ethical violations by 89% (ACM BCB 2025; arXiv 2026).

02 / Retrieval & KGs

Knowledge graphs + RAG for science

Combining knowledge graphs with retrieval-augmented generation for scientific information extraction and question answering.

03 / Data

Animal-science QTL benchmark

Built and annotated a dataset linking quantitative trait locus (QTL) mentions in PubMed to the CMO, VT and LPT livestock trait ontologies.
